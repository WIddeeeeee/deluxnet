Make your own page

  1. Get your login token (register via POST /account/register).
  2. PUT /api/pages/me with {"html":"<h1>Hi!</h1>"} and header Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN.
  3. Visit /users/YOURNAME — that's your page on rec.net!

HTML is sanitized (no scripts). 50KB max, 10 updates/hour. Full guide: WEBPAGE_HOSTING.md.
